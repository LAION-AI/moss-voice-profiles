Emotional trajectory demo grid

Six threshold × step-cap conditions, six concatenated examples per condition and dataset. Mined from the LAION TTS annotated corpus (130.8 M utterances). Companion to corpus_stats.html Part 3.
What you are listening to. Each player is a single speaker's consecutive clips, joined in time order with a 0.35 s gap, so you hear one emotion travelling across 3–5 recordings rather than a single clip. The clips are level-matched to the first one: without that, loudness differences between source recordings are heard as "the change" instead of the emotion.

The two knobs

knobmeaning
T — total changeHow far the emotion moves from first clip to last, in normalised corpus units. 0.25 = crosses a quarter of the corpus on that dimension (calm → noticeably annoyed). 0.50 = a dramatic arc across half the corpus, much rarer.
cap — per-step limitThe largest move any single seam is allowed. This decides how the change is delivered: at 0.10 no cut may move more than a tenth, so the shift must be spread over several clips and sounds like a ramp; at 0.25 the whole shift may happen in one cut, which tends to sound like an edit.
Why the examples in each row are not simply the ones from the row above. The conditions are nested — every chain that passes cap 0.10 also passes 0.20 and 0.25 — so selecting each row by its raw predicate would put the same audio in all three and demonstrate nothing. Each row here is drawn from the band unique to its cap: cap 0.20 means some seam moves more than 0.10, cap 0.25 means some seam moves more than 0.20. The counts printed next to each dataset are the cumulative ones from the exhaustive counting pass, as published in corpus_stats.html — those are the real corpus totals.
Provenance of the examples, stated plainly. The stored trajectory catalogue is capped at EMIT_PER_CELL = 120,000 rows per (bucket, space, k), with the sampling fraction recorded per row in emit_frac. So the catalogue is a capped sample, not the exhaustive set, and the examples below were drawn from it. That is appropriate for choosing demonstrations but it means the catalogue's own row counts are lower than the true totals — which is why the counts shown are taken from the exhaustive pass instead. Rule A throughout (the moving dimension must be the peak emotion of the first or the last clip); emotion space; link mode any.

T ≥ 0.50 · cap 0.10

This condition is empty everywhere, and that is arithmetic rather than a shortage of data. A chain of k clips has k−1 steps, so with a per-step cap of 0.10 the largest reachable total change is 0.10·(k−1). Reaching 0.50 would need k ≥ 6, and the miner only builds chains of up to 5 clips. There is nothing to play: no such trajectory exists in the corpus, at any threshold of effort.
